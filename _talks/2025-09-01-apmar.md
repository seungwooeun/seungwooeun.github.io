A short "Pitch My Work" talk on our Walk-In-Place (WIP) study. I talked about how peripheral optic flow affects unintended positional drift while people walk in place in VR: how its location (left, right, bilateral) and speed create a sense of self-motion (vection), and how bilateral optic flow causes the most lateral drift while one-sided flow tends to suppress it. I also touched on what this means for designing more comfortable, grounded VR locomotion.
