Teaching assistant for **Introduction to Data Science** (course code CB1501017), a second-year undergraduate course in the Computer Science and Engineering program at Pusan National University, taught by Prof. Myungho Lee.

Course overview
======
The course introduces the foundations and applications of data science, and how to frame problems and collect, process, and analyze data to support data-driven decision-making. It covers the interactive and iterative data science process, the quality of data, models, and results within decision-making contexts, computing tools for data science (mainly Python), the statistics and algorithms behind data-driven decisions, and effective data visualization.

Topics
======
* Introduction to data science and its overall process
* Data science tools: spreadsheets and Google Colab
* Exploratory data analysis: basic statistics and visualization
* Statistical inference
* Text analysis and Naive Bayes
* Clustering (k-Means) and classification (k-NN)
* Prediction and regression
* Data collection, cleaning, and integration
* Data visualization (graph processing) and real-world examples with public data

**Tools:** Python (Google Colab)  
**Textbook:** *Computational and Inferential Thinking: The Foundations of Data Science* (2nd ed.), Ani Adhikari, John DeNero, and David Wagner.

As a TA, I supported the labs and assignments, helped students with Python and data analysis, and answered questions throughout the course.
