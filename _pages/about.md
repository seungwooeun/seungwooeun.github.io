I am **Seungwoo Eun**. I work on **3D interaction techniques for Extended Reality (XR)**: how people select and manipulate objects in virtual space, especially content that is far away or at a **scale** that is hard to reach directly.

Much of my work is on **remote interaction**: letting users handle distant objects as easily as nearby ones, even across big differences in **scale**. I build these into working **VR systems** and test them with real users.

I recently finished my **M.S. in Artificial Intelligence** at **Pusan National University** (**XR Lab**, advised by Prof. Myungho Lee), after a **B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering** there in 2024. With that background, I want to use **AI** to make interaction adapt to a user's context and intent, and extend these ideas to **visualization**.
