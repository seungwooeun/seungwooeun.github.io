I am Seungwoo Eun. I work on 3D interaction techniques for Extended Reality (XR). Most of my research is about how people select and manipulate objects in virtual space, especially content that sits far away or at a scale that is hard to reach directly.

A lot of my work focuses on remote interaction: letting users grab, move, and adjust distant objects as easily as nearby ones, and dealing with big differences in scale without losing precision. I build these ideas into working VR systems and test them with real users to see what actually helps.

I recently finished my M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at Pusan National University, in the XR Lab with Prof. Myungho Lee, after a B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the same university in 2024. With that background, I want to use AI to make interaction adapt to a user's context and intent, and bring these ideas to visualization so people can explore complex 3D content more directly.

Feel free to reach out via [email](mailto:aglraswe@gmail.com). I am always up for talking about XR, 3D interaction, AI, or visualization.
